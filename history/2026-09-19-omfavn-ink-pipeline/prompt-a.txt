Top-down view of a sheet of warm handmade paper, the camera locked off and completely still. Burnt sienna ink blooms onto the paper and runs along a single circular brush stroke, starting at the top right and travelling clockwise down the right side, across the bottom and up the left side, closing the open circle in about three seconds, the ink soaking into the paper fibres as it goes. The finished stroke then holds still for the last second. No hand, no brush, nothing else in frame, nothing else moves.
