    About this map

    • A diamond is a documented incident, curated from public reporting since 2018. Tap one for the story and source.
    • A round dot is a live crowd report from the last 24 hours. Dots grow with the number of reports and group into numbered circles when zoomed out.
    • A dim dot is older than 24 hours.
    • Zoomed in, a wedge shows the direction reports agree on; a faint halo means they disagree.

    A dot shows where reports came from, never where a drone was, and it grows with the number of reports, not with certainty. Drone Reporter is a volunteer network in beta, not an official or verified source.
