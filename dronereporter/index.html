Since 2022, unidentified drones have shut airports and circled nuclear sites across Europe. The 2025 wave has not receded: in 2026 NATO jets downed four drones over Alliance soil.

What can you do?

Download Drone Reporter and report any drone you see. Two taps and it's on the map. The more people looking up, the harder Europe is to catch off guard. The app is in beta on iPhone and Android.

What is Drone Reporter?

Drone Reporter is a free app for reporting the drones you see. Photograph one, or hold your phone up and track it live. Two reports of the same drone are enough to place it and trace its path on a map anyone can open.
