omfavn
Closed beta

An AI parenting coachgrounded in your family’s everyday moments.

Record a real moment at home. Omfavn works out what happened in it, and Oline writes back to you about it.

01

Record

A few minutes of an ordinary evening. Dinner, bedtime, the argument about the LEGO.

02

Understand

The recording is transcribed and split by speaker. How something was said is kept next to what was said.

03

Reflect

Oline, the coach in the app, writes back about the moment. She does not ask you questions and she does not expect a reply. It is not a chat.

Where this is now

Omfavn is an iPhone app in Danish. It is still being built and it is not on the App Store.

A closed beta for a small number of families comes next.
